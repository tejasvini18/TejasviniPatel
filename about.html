About Me

Quality engineering with an automation-first mindset.

I’m a passionate Quality Engineer with 5+ years of experience delivering high-quality software through manual and automation testing. I specialise in robust test automation frameworks, API, performance and security testing, and integrating quality into CI/CD pipelines.

My approach

I turn complex requirements into practical test strategies, reusable automation and clear evidence that developers, product owners and stakeholders can act on. I work across the test pyramid and favour automation that is readable, deterministic, maintainable and CI-ready.

My strongest area is web automation with Playwright and TypeScript/Python, supported by Selenium, Cypress, API testing, SQL validation, performance testing, security checks and modern DevOps practices. I also bring experience with C# utilities and legacy automation ecosystems.
